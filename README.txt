RETRO WALKMAN — ANDROID INSTALL GUIDE

1. Upload all files in this package to an HTTPS web host.
2. Open the resulting web address in Chrome on your Android phone.
3. Tap Chrome's three-dot menu.
4. Choose “Install app” or “Add to Home screen”.
5. Confirm Install.
6. Launch Walkman from its home-screen icon.
7. Tap LOAD MUSIC FROM PHONE and select your audio tracks.

A PWA cannot be fully installed directly from a local file:// address.
It needs to be served over HTTPS first.

After the app has opened successfully online, the interface is cached for offline use.
The music files you select remain on your phone; the player does not upload them.
